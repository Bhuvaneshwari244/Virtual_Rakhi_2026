1. **Welcome Screen** 👋
   - Enter brother's name

   - Choose to celebrate together

2. **Tilak Ceremony** 🔴
   - Select from 3 sacred tilak styles

   - Experience warm embrace

   - Download memory card

   - Replay ceremony
